EMERGENCY RESPONSE

Prepared Security Response

Security incidents require clear procedures, communication and appropriate coordination. Oklain Security can support clients with established response procedures for security incidents occurring within designated sites.

Response activities are structured according to the nature of the site, agreed responsibilities and the client's emergency procedures.

Key Activities

  • Receiving and communicating incident information
  • Responding according to agreed procedures
  • Securing affected areas where appropriate
  • Notifying responsible personnel
  • Recording incident information
  • Preparing incident reports
  • Supporting emergency procedures
  • Following client escalation protocols
