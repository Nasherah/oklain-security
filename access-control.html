ACCESS CONTROL

Controlled Access to Your Premises

Access control is an important part of site security. It helps organizations manage who enters, exits and moves through designated areas.

Oklain Security can support access procedures based on the requirements of the site, including visitor management, entry monitoring and security checks.

Key Activities

  • Entry and exit monitoring
  • Visitor registration
  • Verification of authorized access
  • Vehicle access monitoring
  • Identification of unusual activity
  • Access records and reporting
  • Restricted-area monitoring
  • Following client access procedures
